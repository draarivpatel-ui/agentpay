Policy Studio

The gate is yours to configure. Each policy carries a merchant scope, a category scope, an amount ceiling, a velocity window with transaction and spend caps, an allowed UTC hour range, and a minimum trust tier. Toggling or removing a policy appends to the audit trail, and changes apply to the next agent run.

Run The Agent

Atlas is a scripted shopping agent, not a live model. Each run proposes purchases from a seeded synthetic catalog of ten merchants, and every proposal is scored by the gate before it can reach the queue.

Seeded and deterministic. The same run proposes the same purchases.

The Approval Queue

Nothing spends on its own. Proposals wait here until a human decides. Approving runs the simulated checkout stub and writes an approved row to the ledger. Rejecting writes a blocked row. Both append audit entries with before and after states.

How The Engine Reasons

The score starts from a base of 62 and moves with the evidence. Trust tier adjusts it, amount and velocity apply bounded penalties, and each rule hit subtracts weight by the severity of its policy. A policy verdict always wins over the arithmetic.

No enabled policy matches, so the proposal is held for review instead of passing silently.

Hit weight follows the policy action: block 22, review 10, allow 4, unmatched 10. Penalties cap at 40 for hits, 20 for amount, and 24 for velocity.

Verdicts Beat Numbers

A block hit blocks at any score, a review hit holds the proposal for a human, and a clean evaluation still needs a score of 45 or higher to clear on its own.

The Transaction Ledger

Every seed and session transaction in one table. Sort columns and filter by text and number in AG Grid Community, loaded from a CDN. Switch to the built in table to group rows by merchant, category, agent, status and more, with counts and subtotals. If the CDN fails, that table takes over on its own.

Loading the ledger table.

Audit Integrity

Append only and hash chained. Each entry records the actor, the action, the entity, and the before and after states. The chain is reverified on every render. The hash is FNV 1a, chosen for determinism in a demo, not for cryptographic strength.
