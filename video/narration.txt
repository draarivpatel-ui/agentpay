Meet AgentPay, a policy console for shopping agents that spend money on your behalf. As AI agents start making purchases, they need the same guardrails any new spending channel gets. In the policy studio you set ceilings, category rules, velocity limits, time windows, and trust tiers per merchant. Atlas, a simulated shopping agent, proposes purchases against your rules. Every proposal runs through a deterministic risk engine that shows its score, the exact rule hits, and a plain reason for the verdict. Borderline buys wait in an approval queue where you decide with one click. Everything lands in a hash chained audit trail with before and after states you can verify. The ledger is built on AG Grid with sorting, filtering, and grouped views, and a fallback table if the grid cannot load. The checkout step is clearly simulated, with the PayPal sandbox wiring point documented for judges.
